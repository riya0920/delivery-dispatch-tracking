  THE GRID HAS NO DISTANCE DEPENDENCE AND THE REAL CITY DOES.

    wilmington : median 1.75 on the shortest trips -> 1.32 on the
                 longest, a 24% fall
    grid       : median 1.30 -> 1.39, flat

  That is not a small correction and it points the wrong way for this
  project. A barrier or a one-way pair costs a fixed number of metres,
  and a fixed number of metres is a bigger fraction of a short trip.
  DELIVERIES ARE SHORT TRIPS.

  On the shortest bucket the grid says 1.30 and the city says 1.75 --
  it understates the detour by 34% exactly where the business lives.
  The p90 there is 1.52 against 3.03, which is close to double.

  A SINGLE MEAN DETOUR MULTIPLIER IS THE WRONG SHAPE OF CORRECTION.
  The previous pass already said the mean could not be applied to the
  tail; this says it cannot be applied across trip lengths either.

==============================================================================
B. ONE-WAY ASYMMETRY: 7.7% ON THE GRID, AND ON A REAL CITY?
==============================================================================
  pairs where A->B differs from B->A : 85.0%
  mean gap when they differ          : 7.5%
  p90 gap                            : 16.5%

  The synthetic grid measured 7.7%% of routes asymmetric. Wilmington
  is 85%, which is 11x more, on a street network where half the
  ways are one-way (50.6%).

  THE PREVIOUS PASS CALLED THIS A CORRECTNESS BUG AND UNDERSTATED HOW
  OFTEN IT FIRES. An assignment scorer that caches 'the distance
  between a and b' is wrong on 7.7%% of pairs in the grid's world and
  on 85% of them here. That is not an edge case, it is the common
  case, and the direction of travel has to be part of the key.

==============================================================================
WHAT THIS IS NOT
==============================================================================
  ONE CITY, and a mid-sized American one with a river through it. The
  numbers above are Wilmington's, not 'a real metro's' -- Manhattan's
  grid and London's medieval core would both disagree, in opposite
  directions. What transfers is the SHAPE of the error, not its size.

  NO TRAFFIC. OSM does not know what time it is. The time-of-day
  multipliers in the ETA work remain this project's own assumption,
  so the rush-hour results are not validated by anything here -- only
  the geometry is.

  NO TURN RESTRICTIONS. `type=restriction` relations are not parsed,
  so every junction here permits every turn. That makes these detour
  factors a LOWER BOUND on the real ones.

  NOT A ROUTING SERVICE. A* over 15k nodes in-process, no contraction
  hierarchies, no live traffic feed, and the ETA models were not
  refitted on this network.
